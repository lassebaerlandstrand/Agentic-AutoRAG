## Move types

Your `meta.move_type` declares the kind of change you are making. Each move
type has a lever constraint enforced at parse time. If the declared move type
and actual change are inconsistent, your YAML will be rejected and you will be
asked to retry.

- **PROBE** — exactly one primary lever changes. Mandatory for any proposal
  that doesn't have prior evidence backing the combination. Expected delta
  must be non-trivial (≥ 0.03 in absolute value). Use when there is a clear
  bottleneck and no intervention has been confirmed for it yet.
- **REFINE** — up to two primary levers, each within a small step of current
  (chunk_token_size within ±25%, top_k within ±3, hybrid_alpha within ±0.15).
  Discrete primary levers (embedding_model, llm_model, reranker, index_type,
  chunking_strategy, reasoning) cannot change in REFINE. Use when the bottleneck
  is stable and last trial improved.
- **PIVOT** — must change at least one structural lever (index_type,
  embedding_model, or llm_model). Use after ≥ 2 consecutive non-improvements,
  or when every intervention tried for the current bottleneck has been
  falsified. Justify why current trajectory is stuck.
- **COMPOUND** — at least two primary levers change, and each changed lever's
  *proposed value* must have been confirmed in a prior trial (matching on the
  specific model / number, not just the lever name). Use late in the trial
  budget to combine known-good choices.
- **REVERT** — references a specific prior trial_number and applies exactly
  one new primary-lever change relative to that trial. Use after a large
  regression. Set `meta.revert_to_trial` to the baseline trial number.

"Primary levers": index_type, embedding_model, chunking_strategy,
chunk_token_size, reranker, top_k, hybrid_alpha, llm_model, reasoning.
"Secondary levers" (changes to these do NOT count toward the move-type cap):
chunk_token_overlap, reranker_top_n, temperature, query_expansion,
graph_query_mode, graph_top_k.

`meta.primary_lever` must name one of the actually-changed primary levers.
Declaring a primary_lever you didn't change will be rejected.

## Rules

1. Do NOT propose a configuration identical to any prior trial. Check history.
2. Prefer levers from the Diagnoser's `applicable_levers` list, but you may go
   outside it if the knowledge base or history gives you a clear reason —
   explain in `meta.rationale` when you do.
3. `meta.hypothesis` must be a concrete prediction ("swapping X to Y should
   raise target_metric by +delta"); the next Diagnoser will check this
   arithmetically against the stored stage metrics. Lying here costs you the
   next move.
4. `meta.target_metric` must be one of the stage metrics, AND `meta.expected_delta`
   must describe an IMPROVEMENT for that metric (validator rejects regressions).
   All stage metrics are higher-is-better, so `expected_delta` must be positive:
     - retrieval_success: higher is better → expected_delta > 0
     - ranking_quality: higher is better → expected_delta > 0
     - gold_in_reranker_window: higher is better → expected_delta > 0
     - generation_given_context: higher is better → expected_delta > 0
   Example: if you expect a change to raise retrieval_success from 0.70 to
   0.80, write `expected_delta: +0.10`.
5. Update `meta.memo` — ≤ 5 bullets of durable findings you want the next
   Proposer to remember. Carry forward bullets from the latest memo in history
   when they are still true; drop falsified ones. One bullet per durable claim.

## Output

Emit first a short prose explanation (≤ 150 words) of what you are changing
and why, grounded in the knowledge base. Then emit a single ```yaml``` block
containing the flat TrialConfig *followed by* a `meta` dict, exactly like:

```yaml
chunking_strategy: recursive
chunk_token_size: 384
chunk_token_overlap: 48
embedding_model: BAAI/bge-m3
index_type: hybrid_bm25_vector
top_k: 12
hybrid_alpha: 0.5
reranker: BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3
reranker_top_n: 5
query_expansion: none
llm_model: bedrock/global.anthropic.claude-haiku-4-5-20251001-v1:0
temperature: 1.0
reasoning: false
meta:
  move_type: PROBE
  primary_lever: embedding_model
  hypothesis: "swapping embedding_model to BAAI/bge-m3 should raise retrieval_success by +0.08"
  target_metric: retrieval_success
  expected_delta: 0.08
  rationale: "Diagnoser flagged retrieval as the bottleneck; KB gives bge-m3 a higher MTEB retrieval score than the current encoder."
  memo:
    - "BAAI/bge-large-en raised ranking_quality but not retrieval_success (trial 2)"
    - "reasoning=true on o4-mini did not help generation_given_context (trial 3)"
```

Every field above is required. Do NOT nest the TrialConfig under a sub-key.
Do NOT omit the `meta` dict. Do NOT include a `chosen_intervention_idx` field —
that field has been removed.
