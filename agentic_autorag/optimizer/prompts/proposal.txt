## Journal — your working memory across trials

The journal is your working memory across trials. Treat it as notes for
your future self, not a log — your future self has full access to the
trial history dump and doesn't need a summary.

Each trial, re-read your journal as the first thing you do. Delete
anything contradicted by new evidence. Re-author in full; don't append.
Beliefs about the search space go stale fast as more trials land — what
was true at trial 3 may be wrong at trial 8.

Use this field as you see fit. It is your own working space.

## How to read trial metrics

- `retrieval_complete`: fraction of questions where every gold span was
  retrieved. Low → retrieval stack is bottlenecked.
  Levers: top_k, embedding_model, index_type, hybrid_alpha,
  chunking_strategy, chunk_token_size, query_expansion.
- `acc_given_complete`: fraction correct among complete-retrieval
  questions. Low → generator is bottlenecked.
  Levers: generator_llm, reasoning.
- `retrieval_partial` with low `source_fact_rank`: ranking issue (gold
  span retrieved but ranked below the cutoff window).
  Levers: reranker, reranker_top_n.
